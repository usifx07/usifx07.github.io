Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Global Scout Drive is a private, non-commercial tool operated by one individual. It is intended to transfer summaries of public-company accounting research to a designated folder in the operator's own Google Drive.

Data and Google permissions

The operator authorizes Google Drive access using Google's OAuth authorization process. The requested permissions are shown during consent. The software is designed to write and verify its own research-report files within the selected Drive location. Depending on the authorized scope, Google may grant broader technical Drive access than this narrow operational purpose; the operator should review the consent screen carefully.

The exported report format is designed to include public company ticker symbols, identifiers, financial metrics, screening dates, and source-integrity hashes. It is not designed to include financial account numbers, securities positions, Google account data, or credentials.

Storage and disclosure

OAuth credentials are intended to be kept in the operator-controlled server configuration, not on this public website or in a public source-code repository. Reports are stored in the operator's Google Drive and local private server storage. The application does not sell personal data, display advertising, or intentionally share report content with third parties. Google processes Google Drive and authorization data under its own policies.

Retention, access, and revocation

The operator controls the files stored on Google Drive, can delete report copies, and can revoke the application's Google access in Google Account settings. Local application credentials and reports can be removed by the operator. This website does not accept login credentials or provide user registration.

Contact

For privacy questions relating to this privately operated application, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
