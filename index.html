Global Scout Drive

Global Scout Drive is a private, non-commercial utility used by one operator to deliver public-company financial research summaries from an existing research process to the operator's own Google Drive.

What the app does

The tool prepares limited accounting-screening reports based on public U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission company data. With the operator's explicit Google authorization, it can upload reports to a designated folder and verify the uploaded file content. The application is intended for the operator's own account only and is not offered as a public service.

It does not execute investments, trade securities, transfer money, or provide access to brokerage accounts.
